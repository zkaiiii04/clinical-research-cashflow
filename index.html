01 — The rules

How each piece should behave

Levers, not bans. When something looks off, one of these is being pulled the wrong way.

Up top — quiet the frame

  • V, scoop & soft cowl necks — modest with a cami, and they narrow the line.
  • Set-in or raglan sleeves; ¾ sleeves ending at the waist quietly mark it.
  • Detail at the bust — ruching, texture, a pocket.
  • Front-tuck almost everything.
  • Shoulder pads, statement puffs, boat & off-shoulder necks.
  • Boxy crops ending at the widest part of the hip.

Down low — lean into volume

  • High rise, always — supports the seat, defines the waist, adds leg.
  • Wide-leg, straight, bootcut, flare — skim the slimmer thigh, balance the shoulders.
  • A-line & bias midis — flatter the seat, dodge the waistband gap entirely.
  • Curve-cut denim — contoured band, higher back rise.
  • Rigid low-rise straight-size denim — the cause of the back gap.
  • Tight skinny in stiff fabric — clings at the seat, empty at the thigh.

The hourglass playbook

Hourglass = shoulder line ≈ hip line with a visibly narrower waist. You have the first part; these manufacture the second:

  • Wrap everything: wrap dresses and tops are your single most reliable garment.
  • Belt what can be belted — blazers, cardigans, coats. Tonal = sleek, contrast = focal point.
  • High rise + tuck = instant waist; half-tuck when a full one feels stiff.
  • Nipped tailoring — visible waist suppression, never boxy. A fitted waistcoat is the pad-free cheat.
  • Fitted top over volume bottom — the small-top / big-bottom ratio is the hourglass ratio.
  • Tonal column + one waist marker — lengthens 163 cm, then carves the middle.

Silhouette glossary

Between sizes? Wraps, smocked waists, knits, A-line, and denim with 2–5% stretch move with a changing body. Buy bottoms for the seat; taking in a waistband is a five-minute tailoring job.

03 — Fabric

The low-polyester cheat sheet

Keep polyester and nylon off your skin; accept a little stretch where fit needs it. 90–95% natural + 5–10% elastane still cuts ~90% of the microplastics.

LinenThe Singapore MVP — breathable, modest without heat, better with age. Wrinkles are the look.
Organic cottonEveryday base; look for GOTS. Blends fine for fitted pieces.
HempDurable, antimicrobial, wicking. Usually blended with cotton.
TENCEL lyocellSilky drape, wicks sweat, closed-loop made. Slips and activewear.
Merino woolTravel hero — thermoregulating, odour-resistant, packs tiny, re-wears.
SilkOccasion drape. Check for peace-silk if sourcing matters to you.

Read the label

  • Skip 100% polyester, especially base layers.
  • Accept 2–10% elastane; bio-based ROICA where possible.
  • Grey zone: viscose/rayon/bamboo viscose are plant-based but dirty to process; closed-loop lyocell is the clean version.
  • Trust: GOTS · OEKO-TEX 100 · Bluesign · Fair Trade · B-Corp

05 — Gym

The honest activewear section

Almost all activewear is synthetic. The realistic move: a mostly-natural base (TENCEL, organic cotton, merino) with a small % of stretch — not chasing zero.

TENCEL + elastaneOrganic cottonMerino

  • Biker shorts + longline top — modest and current.

07 — Shoes

Heels you can actually walk in

Sweet spot: 4–5.5 cm block or kitten heel, almond or square toe — leg-lengthening at 163 cm, all-day walkable, zero grandma. Try them in the outfit builder above.

Comfy, not frumpy

  • Almond or square toe — square reads current.
  • Slingback, ankle strap or d'Orsay for stability.
  • Cushioned footbed + arch support in pliable leather.
  • Heel set under your heel, not far back.
  • Chunky comfort-clogs, beige rounded-toe pumps — the actual frumpy zone.
  • Skinny stilettos, if walking matters.

For your shape

Low heel + almond toe under wide-legs or a midi = one long line, waist to floor. Slingback + cropped denim = easy smart-casual. Keep shoes tonal so the bottoms stay the story. Good leather resoles — invest like workwear.

08 — Jewellery

Pieces that never tarnish

For never-take-off jewellery, material is everything — it decides survival in SG humidity. Best to worst:

Solid gold 14k+ & platinumGenuinely never tarnishes. Zero maintenance, heirloom-grade. For the everyday stack.
Thick gold vermeil2.5-micron gold on recycled sterling (5× industry minimum). Years of wear; keep from perfume/chlorine.
Stainless steel / titaniumWaterproof, anti-tarnish, cheap — shower and swim without thinking.
Avoid: gold-plated brassPlating wears fast, brass marks skin. The "cheap and ruined" trap.
Your move: a never-remove stack in solid gold (studs, huggies, one thin chain), then steel or vermeil for trends. Good brands now use 95%+ recycled gold — non-tarnish and low-impact.

10 — Travel & the four seasons

Packing from a tropical base

Don't own a winter wardrobe — own layers that stack onto your SG capsule. Merino does the heavy lifting.

Spring · mild + wet

Linen-blend trousers, breathable knits, cotton/TENCEL trench, one packable cardigan.

Summer · temperate

Your SG wardrobe, easier — linen dresses, cotton tees, wide linen legs.

Autumn · cool

Merino base layers, wool knits, denim, wool-blend coat, scarf.

Winter · cold

Merino base layer (thin, warm, packs to nothing) + wool coat + thermal leggings under trousers or a midi.

Capsule: one neutral palette — 1 coat · 2–3 knits · merino bases · wide trousers · 1–2 dresses · one curve denim · versatile shoes. Merino re-wears without washing; roll the linen for warm legs of the trip.
