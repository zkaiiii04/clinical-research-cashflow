What to look at

  • Switch between the two projects and click Load Project. The month, quarter and FY columns change to fit each project's dates.
  • Click − on a quarter header to collapse it to its total; + expands it again.
  • Type in the yellow cells. Totals, subtotals, variances and the KPI cards update straight away.
  • Variance compares actuals with projections only up to the Latest SOA Month, so planned spend in later months is not counted as underspend.
  • 3D Gait has saved projections, including one against an old line name ("Publications") and one in a month after the project end. They appear as Unmatched rows, are counted in the totals, and can be re-assigned or deleted.
  • Esko Combo has one manpower line charged to OM (shown in the OM block) and one manpower line with no matching rate.
  • The IO · Code column follows the manpower IO convention, with the budget code joined to the IO and no space (e.g. TF25HIP136OM). It is merged across consecutive lines that share it, so MP budget lines and manpower lines on TF25HIP136MP share one cell.
  • Locked manpower rows end in “(Projected)” so they stand apart from the MP budget lines.
  • Variance uses the standard number format (negatives in red brackets), with no extra colour coding.
  • Sample figures: the 3D Gait totals match the reconciled checkpoints ($250,000 budget, $68,563.67 actuals, $62,250 RA projection), but how they are spread across months is made up. Esko Combo uses the budget lines and actuals saved in v0.8; its manpower lines are made up. The two projects are shown with the same Latest SOA Month for simplicity.
